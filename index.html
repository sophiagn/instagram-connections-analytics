Instagram Connections Analyzer

Find out who doesn't follow you back by uploading your Instagram followers and following data.

Follow these steps to export the two JSON files this app needs.

Step 1

Go to your profile and open Settings and privacy.

Step 2

Open Accounts Center, then your information and permissions.

Step 3

Click export your information, create export.

Step 4

Choose to export to device.

Step 5

Click customize information. Uncheck all options except for "Followers and following" under Connections.

Step 6

Change the date range to "All time".

Step 7

Change format type to JSON.

Step 8

Start export. Wait a few minutes until you receive an email from Instagram telling you your files are available for download.

Step 9

Download your data from the email link provided by Instagram.

Step 10

You may need to extract the downloaded files if they are in a compressed format. You should be able to find followers_1.json (plus any additional followers_*.json files if you have several thousand followers) and following.json at /connections/followers_and_following.
